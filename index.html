00Summary

A self-contained dash unit that measures wheel speed with a Hall sensor on a non-driven wheel, adds GPS for lap and position, and shows the driver the one thing that wins an Eco-marathon attempt: whether their average speed is on course to finish inside the time limit. It runs from a single 18650 cell for a full race weekend, logs everything to microSD for post-attempt strategy, and makes no connection to the motor controller or propulsion wiring.

01Assumptions

Shell Eco-marathon prototype, battery-electric category. The attempt is a fixed number of laps inside a time limit, so the driver's job is pacing: meet the minimum average speed using the least energy.

Auxiliary batteries are permitted for ancillary electronics (the rules on chemistry and energy cap are listed under Integration as things to confirm). Non-contact sensing near propulsion wiring is acceptable; any galvanic connection to the motor controller is not. The front wheels are non-driven and accessible for magnets. A race day involves four to six hours of powered-on time including practice sessions.

02Displayed fields and why

SpeedHall sensor, GPS cross-check

Required. Large digits, from the Hall sensor, cross-checked by GPS. Smoothed with a short exponential filter so the digits do not flicker.

Average speed vs minimumComputed, distance / elapsed

The most useful number on the screen. It tells the driver directly whether they can afford to coast for longer or need to burn.

Lap, lap time, deltaGPS geofence, button backup

Current lap number out of the total, the running lap time, and the last lap's delta against the target lap time. This turns pacing into something the driver can act on each lap.

Elapsed and remainingMCU timer

When a lap goes wrong, through traffic or a slow car ahead, the driver needs the clock to decide how hard to recover.

Strategy cueLap distance from Hall pulses

"BURN" or "GLIDE" with the metres to the next change, from a burn-and-glide plan stored per track (where to accelerate, where to coast). Most of the energy in an Eco-marathon is won or lost here, and a plan the driver can see beats one they have to remember.

Pace cue LEDProjected finish time, with hysteresis

Green for coast, amber for hold, red for burn, in peripheral vision so eyes stay on the track. Driven by the projected finish time at the current average.

DistanceHall pulse count

Confirms the lap count if a start-line crossing is missed.

System healthADC, GPS, SD status

Aux battery voltage, GPS satellite count and SD logging status on the bottom line. A dash that dies mid-attempt is worse than no dash.

Propulsion current and energy are not shown. The brief forbids a motor controller connection. A clamp-on Hall current sensor around a propulsion cable would be non-contact, but whether that counts as a connection is a rules question rather than an assumption to make.

03How each measurement is obtained

Speed and distance

Four neodymium magnets on the front hub in opposing pairs (so the wheel stays balanced), and a DRV5023 digital Hall sensor on the upright (it runs from 2.5 V; the common A3144 needs 4.5 V and would not work on a 3.3 V rail) with a 1 to 2 mm gap. The microcontroller timestamps each pulse in an interrupt and measures the period between pulses rather than counting pulses per second, which gives fine resolution at 15 km/h. Speed is circumference divided by four times the period; distance is the pulse count times a quarter circumference. The loaded rolling circumference is calibrated by pushing the car with the driver aboard over a measured 10 m. A 2 s timeout shows zero. A hardware RC filter and a Schmitt-trigger input, plus a 15 ms minimum period in software, reject bounce and motor EMI.

Position and laps

A u-blox M10 receiver at 10 Hz. Laps are detected by a geofence around the start/finish line, with a tiller button as manual backup and a 60 s minimum lap time to block double triggers. GPS speed is logged alongside wheel speed so circumference drift can be checked after each session.

Time and battery

Elapsed time comes from the MCU timer, started by the first start-line crossing or the lap button. Battery voltage comes from a 100k/100k divider into ADC1 at 11 dB attenuation: 4.2 V reads 2.1 V and 3.0 V reads 1.5 V, both inside the calibrated 0 to 3.1 V range. A 100 nF capacitor gives the ADC a low source impedance.

One protected 3.6 V 18650 Li-ion cell (over-discharge cut at 2.8 V, over-current and short protection on the cell's own board). After protection and temperature derating about 2.6 Ah is usable, which gives roughly 30 h of continuous running (2.6 Ah / 87 mA): more than five times a long race day, with margin for cold mornings and cell ageing. A single cell keeps the pack at around 50 g and 10.8 Wh, cheap, and charged outside the car in a standard charger, with a spare cell rotated in. Even at 0 °C and 80 % ageing it still runs for over 20 h.

A buck-boost regulator is used rather than an LDO because the cell runs from 4.2 V down to 3.0 V across a 3.3 V rail. If the rules push toward a safer chemistry the same design accepts a 2S LiFePO4 pack at 6.4 V with no change beyond the regulator's input range. Four AA alkalines would also last a weekend but need replacing and sag in the cold.

06Installation and operating conditions

The dash unit sits in an IP54 ABS box on the steering tiller or bulkhead, in the driver's lower eyeline, foam-mounted to damp vibration. Prototype cars have little suspension and a hot cabin, so the design assumes 0 to 55 °C, sustained vibration and occasional moisture.

All external wiring is twisted pair, routed on the opposite side of the chassis from the motor and controller cables, and strain-relieved at both ends. The Hall sensor is potted in epoxy on a small bracket and its cable shield is bonded to ground at the dash end only. Magnets are glued and mechanically retained. The GPS antenna goes on top of the body, because a carbon shell blocks the signal. The battery is accessible without tools and fused at the pack. Mass budget is about 150 g excluding cables.

08Information needed for integration

Rules on auxiliary batteries: permitted chemistries, energy cap, fusing and isolation, and whether non-contact current sensing counts as a motor controller connection. Whether wireless telemetry to the pits is allowed during attempts.

From the car: loaded tyre circumference, hub geometry and space for a sensor bracket, body material for antenna placement, the driver's seating position and sightline, available dash area, and whether the driver can reach buttons in the harness. From the event: lap length, lap count, time limit and the start-line coordinates.

09Circuit schematics

Sheet 1 covers power and the sensor front-ends. A P-FET guards against a reversed cell, the battery divider is taken after the power switch so it draws nothing when off, and a fixed-output TPS63021 holds 3.3 V across the cell's whole 4.2 to 3.0 V range. The Hall output has a 10k pull-up, a 1k/10 nF RC with a 10 µs time constant, and a BAT54S clamp to both rails before the GPIO.

Sheet 2 is the microcontroller and its peripherals. The LCD and SD card share one hardware SPI bus with separate chip selects. Pins avoid the S3's strapping pins (0, 3, 45, 46), native USB (19, 20) and the PSRAM pins. The memory LCD needs its VCOM polarity flipped about once a second to avoid DC across the panel; with EMD tied low this happens in the SPI command byte on every refresh, and a loop watchdog reboots the MCU if the firmware ever stalls.

10Firmware

Arduino sketch for the ESP32-S3 (also in the repository). Speed comes from an interrupt that records the pulse period; lap detection is a GPS geofence with a button backup; the pace cue compares the projected finish time with the time limit; the strategy cue reads the lap distance against the plan in config.h; everything is logged to CSV at 10 Hz. All race and vehicle assumptions live in config.h. Libraries: Adafruit_SharpMem, Adafruit_GFX, TinyGPSPlus, SD.

config.h race and vehicle parameters
// Race and vehicle parameters. Everything here is an assumption until the team
// confirms it; see "Information needed for integration" in the proposal.
#pragma once
#include <stdint.h>

// Wheel: loaded rolling circumference with the driver aboard, measured by
// pushing the car over 10 m and counting pulses.
constexpr float WHEEL_CIRC_M     = 1.60f;   // ~20 inch wheel, to be measured
constexpr float MAGNETS_PER_REV  = 4.0f;

// Track (placeholder values, set per event)
constexpr double START_LAT       = 51.5000;
constexpr double START_LON       = -0.1200;
constexpr float  START_RADIUS_M  = 12.0f;   // geofence radius for lap detection
constexpr float  LAP_M           = 1500.0f;
constexpr uint8_t TOTAL_LAPS     = 10;
constexpr uint32_t TIME_LIMIT_S  = 36 * 60; // attempt time limit
constexpr float  MIN_AVG_KMH     = (TOTAL_LAPS * LAP_M / 1000.0f) / (TIME_LIMIT_S / 3600.0f);
constexpr uint32_t TARGET_LAP_MS = (uint32_t)(TIME_LIMIT_S * 1000.0f / TOTAL_LAPS * 0.97f); // 3 % safety
constexpr uint32_t MIN_LAP_MS    = 60 * 1000;  // ignore lap triggers closer than this

// Pace cue thresholds, in seconds of slack on the projected finish time
constexpr float PACE_HIGH_S = 60.0f;   // more slack than this: coast
constexpr float PACE_LOW_S  = 20.0f;   // less slack than this: burn
constexpr float PACE_HYST_S = 10.0f;

// Strategy plan: where on the lap each burn or coast starts, in metres from
// the start line. Flat-track placeholder from the simulator; the real plan
// comes from practice-run logs on the actual circuit (hills move the burns).
struct PlanStep { float fromM; bool burn; };
constexpr PlanStep PLAN[] = {
  {   0.0f, true  },   // burn out of the start line
  { 100.0f, false },   // long glide
  { 750.0f, true  },   // second burn
  { 850.0f, false },   // glide to the line
};
constexpr int PLAN_STEPS = sizeof(PLAN) / sizeof(PLAN[0]);

// ---------------------------------------------------------------- pins
// Chosen to avoid strapping pins (0, 3, 45, 46), native USB (19, 20),
// UART0 (43, 44) and the octal PSRAM pins (35 to 37).
constexpr int PIN_VBAT      = 1;   // ADC1_CH0, 100k/100k divider
constexpr int PIN_BTN_LAP   = 4;
constexpr int PIN_BTN_PAGE  = 5;
constexpr int PIN_BTN_RESET = 6;
constexpr int PIN_PULSE     = 7;   // Hall sensor, falling edge
constexpr int PIN_LCD_CS    = 10;  // Sharp CS is active high; the library handles it
constexpr int PIN_MOSI      = 11;
constexpr int PIN_SCK       = 12;
constexpr int PIN_MISO      = 13;
constexpr int PIN_SD_CS     = 14;
constexpr int PIN_LCD_DISP  = 16;  // high = display on
constexpr int PIN_GPS_RX    = 17;  // from GPS TX
constexpr int PIN_GPS_TX    = 18;  // to GPS RX
constexpr int PIN_LED_G     = 39;
constexpr int PIN_LED_R     = 40;

// ---------------------------------------------------------------- speed from Hall pulses
// The ISR records the time between successive pulses. Period measurement, not
// pulse counting, so resolution is good at low speed. Pulses closer than
// MIN_PERIOD_US are treated as bounce or EMI (that is ~96 km/h here).
volatile uint32_t g_lastPulseUs  = 0;
volatile uint32_t g_periodUs     = 0;
volatile uint32_t g_pulseCount   = 0;
constexpr uint32_t MIN_PERIOD_US = 15000;

void IRAM_ATTR onWheelPulse() {
  uint32_t now = micros();
  uint32_t dt  = now - g_lastPulseUs;
  if (dt < MIN_PERIOD_US) return;
  g_lastPulseUs = now;
  g_periodUs    = dt;
  g_pulseCount++;
}

float wheelSpeedKmh() {
  uint32_t period, last;
  noInterrupts();
  period = g_periodUs;
  last   = g_lastPulseUs;
  interrupts();
  // No pulse for 2 s means stopped
  if (period == 0 || (micros() - last) > 2000000UL) return 0.0f;
  float metresPerPulse = WHEEL_CIRC_M / MAGNETS_PER_REV;
  return metresPerPulse / (period * 1e-6f) * 3.6f;
}

float distanceM() {
  uint32_t n;
  noInterrupts();
  n = g_pulseCount;
  interrupts();
  return n * (WHEEL_CIRC_M / MAGNETS_PER_REV);
}

// ---------------------------------------------------------------- race state
struct RaceState {
  bool     running   = false;
  bool     finished  = false;
  uint32_t startMs   = 0;
  uint32_t finishMs  = 0;
  uint32_t lapStartMs = 0;
  uint8_t  lap       = 0;      // completed laps
  float    lapDistStartM = 0;
  float    distStartM = 0;
  uint32_t lastLapMs = 0;
  bool     insideStartZone = false;
} race;

uint8_t page = 0;   // 0 = race, 1 = diagnostics

// Exponentially smoothed speed for the display, raw speed for logging
float speedKmh = 0, speedSmooth = 0;

float batteryVolts() {
  // 11 dB attenuation: about 0 to 3.1 V, calibrated by analogReadMilliVolts.
  // 100k/100k divider, so 4.2 V reads 2.1 V.
  uint32_t mv = 0;
  for (int i = 0; i < 8; i++) mv += analogReadMilliVolts(PIN_VBAT);
  return (mv / 8) / 1000.0f * 2.0f;
}

// ---------------------------------------------------------------- buttons with debounce
struct Button {
  int pin; bool state = true; bool last = true; uint32_t tChange = 0; bool pressed = false;
  void update() {
    bool r = digitalRead(pin);
    if (r != last) { tChange = millis(); last = r; }
    if ((millis() - tChange) > 30 && r != state) { state = r; if (!state) pressed = true; }
  }
  bool take() { bool p = pressed; pressed = false; return p; }
};
Button btnLap{PIN_BTN_LAP}, btnPage{PIN_BTN_PAGE}, btnReset{PIN_BTN_RESET};

// ---------------------------------------------------------------- lap and pacing logic
void startRace() {
  race = RaceState();
  race.running = true;
  race.startMs = millis();
  race.lapStartMs = race.startMs;
  race.lapDistStartM = distanceM();
  race.distStartM = race.lapDistStartM;
  race.insideStartZone = true;
}

void markLap() {
  uint32_t now = millis();
  if (race.finished) return;
  if (!race.running) { startRace(); return; }
  if (now - race.lapStartMs < MIN_LAP_MS) return;    // reject double trigger
  race.lastLapMs = now - race.lapStartMs;
  race.lapStartMs = now;
  race.lap++;
  race.lapDistStartM = distanceM();
  if (race.lap >= TOTAL_LAPS) { race.finished = true; race.running = false; race.finishMs = now; }
}

// Geofence on the start/finish line: fire once when entering the zone
void checkGpsLap() {
  if (!gps.location.isValid() || gps.location.age() > 1500) return;
  double dist = TinyGPSPlus::distanceBetween(gps.location.lat(), gps.location.lng(),
                                             START_LAT, START_LON);
  bool inside = dist < START_RADIUS_M;
  if (inside && !race.insideStartZone) markLap();
  race.insideStartZone = inside;
}

float lapDistM() { return distanceM() - race.lapDistStartM; }

// Strategy cue: current plan action and metres until the next change
struct Cue { bool burn; float toNextM; };
Cue planCue() {
  float d = fmodf(lapDistM(), LAP_M);
  int i = 0;
  while (i + 1 < PLAN_STEPS && d >= PLAN[i + 1].fromM) i++;
  float next = (i + 1 < PLAN_STEPS) ? PLAN[i + 1].fromM : LAP_M;
  return { PLAN[i].burn, next - d };
}

// Pace cue. Compare the projected finish time at the current average speed
// with the time limit. Hysteresis stops the LED flickering between states.
enum Pace { COAST, HOLD, BURN };
Pace pace = HOLD;

Pace computePace(float avgKmh) {
  if (!race.running || avgKmh < 1.0f) return HOLD;
  float remainingKm = TOTAL_LAPS * LAP_M / 1000.0f - (distanceM() - race.distStartM) / 1000.0f;
  float elapsedS = (millis() - race.startMs) / 1000.0f;
  float projectedS = elapsedS + (remainingKm / avgKmh) * 3600.0f;
  float margin = TIME_LIMIT_S - projectedS;        // seconds of slack
  switch (pace) {
    case COAST: return (margin < PACE_LOW_S)  ? HOLD : COAST;
    case BURN:  return (margin > PACE_HIGH_S) ? HOLD : BURN;
    default:
      if (margin > PACE_HIGH_S + PACE_HYST_S) return COAST;
      if (margin < PACE_LOW_S  - PACE_HYST_S) return BURN;
      return HOLD;
  }
}

void setPaceLed(Pace p) {
  digitalWrite(PIN_LED_G, p != BURN);
  digitalWrite(PIN_LED_R, p != COAST);
}

void fmtTime(char* buf, size_t n, uint32_t ms) {
  uint32_t s = ms / 1000;
  snprintf(buf, n, "%02lu:%02lu", (unsigned long)(s / 60), (unsigned long)(s % 60));
}

void drawRacePage(float avgKmh, uint32_t elapsedMs) {
  char buf[32];
  lcd.clearDisplay();
  lcd.setTextColor(BLACK);

  // Top line: lap and elapsed
  lcd.setTextSize(3);
  snprintf(buf, sizeof buf, "LAP %02u/%02u", race.lap + (race.running ? 1 : 0), TOTAL_LAPS);
  lcd.setCursor(8, 6); lcd.print(buf);
  fmtTime(buf, sizeof buf, elapsedMs);
  lcd.setCursor(290, 6); lcd.print(buf);

  // Speed, large, one decimal
  lcd.setTextSize(9);
  snprintf(buf, sizeof buf, "%4.1f", speedSmooth);
  lcd.setCursor(4, 44); lcd.print(buf);
  lcd.setTextSize(2);
  lcd.setCursor(222, 44); lcd.print("km/h");

  // Strategy cue box, reverse video while burning
  Cue c = planCue();
  if (c.burn) lcd.fillRect(300, 40, 96, 74, BLACK);
  else        lcd.drawRect(300, 40, 96, 74, BLACK);
  lcd.setTextColor(c.burn ? WHITE : BLACK);
  lcd.setTextSize(3);
  lcd.setCursor(306, 50); lcd.print(c.burn ? "BURN" : "GLIDE");
  lcd.setTextSize(2);
  snprintf(buf, sizeof buf, "%4.0fm", c.toNextM);
  lcd.setCursor(318, 88); lcd.print(buf);
  lcd.setTextColor(BLACK);

  // Average vs required minimum, and last-lap delta to target
  lcd.setTextSize(3);
  snprintf(buf, sizeof buf, "AVG %4.1f", avgKmh);
  lcd.setCursor(8, 132); lcd.print(buf);
  snprintf(buf, sizeof buf, "MIN %4.1f", MIN_AVG_KMH);
  lcd.setCursor(222, 132); lcd.print(buf);
  if (race.lastLapMs) {
    int32_t deltaMs = (int32_t)race.lastLapMs - (int32_t)TARGET_LAP_MS;
    snprintf(buf, sizeof buf, "LAST %+5.1fs", deltaMs / 1000.0f);
    lcd.setCursor(8, 166); lcd.print(buf);
  }
  int32_t remS = (int32_t)TIME_LIMIT_S - (int32_t)(elapsedMs / 1000);
  snprintf(buf, sizeof buf, "REM %02ld:%02ld", (long)(remS / 60), (long)(labs(remS) % 60));
  lcd.setCursor(222, 166); lcd.print(buf);

  // Status line
  lcd.setTextSize(2);
  snprintf(buf, sizeof buf, "%s %.2fV SAT%lu %s",
           pace == BURN ? "BURN " : pace == COAST ? "COAST" : "HOLD ",
           batteryVolts(), (unsigned long)gps.satellites.value(), sdOk ? "LOG" : "NO SD");
  lcd.setCursor(8, 218); lcd.print(buf);
  lcd.refresh();   // also flips VCOM in the command byte (EXTMODE tied low)
}

// ---------------------------------------------------------------- logging
void openLog() {
  if (!SD.begin(PIN_SD_CS, SPI, 20000000)) { sdOk = false; return; }
  char name[20];
  for (int i = 0; i < 999; i++) {
    snprintf(name, sizeof name, "/run%03d.csv", i);
    if (!SD.exists(name)) break;
  }
  logFile = SD.open(name, FILE_WRITE);
  sdOk = (bool)logFile;
  if (sdOk) logFile.println("t_ms,speed_kmh,gps_kmh,dist_m,lap,lap_ms,lat,lon,sats,vbat,pace,plan_burn");
}

void logRow(uint32_t t) {
  if (!sdOk) return;
  logFile.printf("%lu,%.2f,%.2f,%.1f,%u,%lu,%.6f,%.6f,%lu,%.2f,%d,%d\n",
                 (unsigned long)t, speedKmh, gps.speed.kmph(), distanceM(), race.lap,
                 (unsigned long)(race.running ? millis() - race.lapStartMs : 0),
                 gps.location.lat(), gps.location.lng(), (unsigned long)gps.satellites.value(),
                 batteryVolts(), (int)pace, (int)planCue().burn);
  static uint32_t lastFlush = 0;
  if (millis() - lastFlush > 2000) { logFile.flush(); lastFlush = millis(); }
}

  pinMode(PIN_BTN_RESET, INPUT_PULLUP);
  pinMode(PIN_LED_R, OUTPUT);
  pinMode(PIN_LED_G, OUTPUT);
  pinMode(PIN_LCD_DISP, OUTPUT);
  digitalWrite(PIN_LCD_DISP, LOW);           // blank until the first frame is drawn
  analogSetPinAttenuation(PIN_VBAT, ADC_11db);

  attachInterrupt(digitalPinToInterrupt(PIN_PULSE), onWheelPulse, FALLING);

  SPI.begin(PIN_SCK, PIN_MISO, PIN_MOSI);
  lcd.begin();
  lcd.clearDisplay();
  lcd.refresh();
  digitalWrite(PIN_LCD_DISP, HIGH);

  gpsSerial.begin(115200, SERIAL_8N1, PIN_GPS_RX, PIN_GPS_TX);
  openLog();

  // Power-on self test: both LEDs for 500 ms
  setPaceLed(HOLD); delay(500); setPaceLed(COAST);

  // If the loop ever stalls, reboot rather than freeze the display with DC
  // across the panel. Race state is rebuilt from the next lap button press.
  enableLoopWDT();
}

void loop() {
  static uint32_t lastTick = 0, lastDraw = 0;

  while (gpsSerial.available()) gps.encode(gpsSerial.read());

  btnLap.update(); btnPage.update(); btnReset.update();
  if (btnLap.take())   markLap();
  if (btnPage.take())  page ^= 1;
  if (btnReset.take()) { race = RaceState(); pace = HOLD; }

  uint32_t now = millis();
  if (now - lastTick >= 100) {               // 10 Hz control and logging
    lastTick = now;
    speedKmh = wheelSpeedKmh();
    speedSmooth += 0.3f * (speedKmh - speedSmooth);

    checkGpsLap();

    uint32_t elapsedMs = race.running ? now - race.startMs
                       : race.finished ? race.finishMs - race.startMs : 0;
    float avgKmh = (elapsedMs > 5000)
                   ? (distanceM() - race.distStartM) / 1000.0f / (elapsedMs / 3600000.0f) : 0;
    if (race.finished) avgKmh = TOTAL_LAPS * LAP_M / 1000.0f / (elapsedMs / 3600000.0f);
    pace = computePace(avgKmh);
    setPaceLed(pace);
    logRow(now);

    if (now - lastDraw >= 250) {             // 4 Hz refresh is plenty for a memory LCD
      lastDraw = now;
      if (page == 0) drawRacePage(avgKmh, elapsedMs); else drawDiagPage();
    }
  }
}

"""Bench model of the pulse-period speed calculation and the pace logic.
Run before flashing to sanity-check config values: python3 test/speed_logic_test.py"""

WHEEL_CIRC_M, MAGNETS = 1.60, 4
LAP_M, LAPS, LIMIT_S = 1500, 10, 36 * 60
MIN_PERIOD_US = 15000


def speed_kmh(period_us):
    return (WHEEL_CIRC_M / MAGNETS) / (period_us * 1e-6) * 3.6


def pulse_hz(kmh):
    return (kmh / 3.6) / (WHEEL_CIRC_M / MAGNETS)


# 1. Decoding is exact and the bounce filter does not clip real speeds
for kmh in (5, 15, 25, 35, 60):
    period = 1e6 / pulse_hz(kmh)
    assert abs(speed_kmh(period) - kmh) < 1e-9
    assert period > MIN_PERIOD_US, f"{kmh} km/h would be rejected as bounce"
    print(f"{kmh:>3} km/h -> {pulse_hz(kmh):6.2f} Hz on the signal generator, period {period:8.0f} us")

# 2. Resolution: one microsecond of timer jitter at 35 km/h
p = 1e6 / pulse_hz(35)
print(f"resolution at 35 km/h: {speed_kmh(p) - speed_kmh(p + 1):.5f} km/h per us")

# 3. Pace logic: projected finish against the limit
def margin(elapsed_s, dist_m):
    avg = dist_m / elapsed_s
    return LIMIT_S - (elapsed_s + (LAPS * LAP_M - dist_m) / avg)

min_avg = LAPS * LAP_M / 1000 / (LIMIT_S / 3600)
print(f"minimum average {min_avg:.2f} km/h")
assert abs(margin(600, 600 * min_avg / 3.6)) < 1e-6       # exactly on the limit
assert margin(600, 600 * 26 / 3.6) > 60                    # 26 km/h: coast
assert margin(600, 600 * 24 / 3.6) < 0                     # 24 km/h: burn
print("all checks passed")
